# Internal Communications & Reporting

## 🎯 Purpose
Guidelines for writing effective internal communications (status reports, project updates, newsletters).

## 🛠️ The Process / Fact

### 1. Common Formats
- **3P Update:** Progress, Plans, Problems.
- **Company Newsletter:** Broad updates, announcements.
- **FAQ:** Answering frequently asked questions clearly.

### 2. Strategy
- **Audience First:** Match tone to the audience (leadership vs. teammates).
- **Structure:** Use the appropriate template (e.g., `examples/3p-updates.md`).

## ⚠️ Known Quirks or Edge Cases
- **Genericism:** Avoid vague updates. Be specific about blockers and "plans."
